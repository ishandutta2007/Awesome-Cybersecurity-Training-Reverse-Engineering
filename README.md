## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[OffSec Proving Grounds](https://www.offsec.com/products/proving-grounds/)**  

  **Realistic penetration testing lab environments** for all skill levels . **PG Play** (free) offers **50+ training labs** with dedicated machines designed by the VulnHub community, limited to **3 daily hours** . **PG Practice** ($19/mo or $199/yr) provides **200+ lab environments** including **Windows and Linux machines**, **unlimited time**, and **retired OSCP+ exam labs** for certification prep . Uses an **in-browser Kali Linux instance**—no local setup required . Only **one lab machine at a time** for private, dedicated access .



- **[Hack The Box Enterprise](https://www.hackthebox.com/)**  

  **Dedicated cybersecurity training labs and talent search services** for Fortune 500 corporations, consulting firms, and government agencies . **Dedicated Labs** provide exclusive machines with **two exclusive Machines monthly**, **custom machine uploads**, and **private VPN servers** . **Professional Labs** simulate realistic corporate attack surfaces with **wipe/redeploy on command** and **company-wide certificates** . **Cloud Labs** cover **PaaS and IaaS vulnerabilities** on real cloud infrastructure . **Academy Labs** provide guided training with **unlimited certification attempts** . **Talent Search** connects companies with **1M+ security professionals** filtered by skill matrix and geography . Maps to **MITRE ATT&CK** techniques .



- **[TryHackMe Business](https://tryhackme.com/)**  

  **Gamified cybersecurity training** with a focus on accessibility for beginners . **B2B/EDU packages** add **Insights dashboard** for progress tracking, **content assignment with deadlines**, **custom learning paths** from existing or own material, and **flexible license seating** throughout the contract . **CTF builder** (self-serve) and **tailor-made CTF** options available . Includes **onboarding and ongoing support** from Customer Success and Technical Support .



- **[Cybrary for Business](https://www.cybrary.it/)**  

  **Hands-on cybersecurity training** with **Baselining tool** to identify and remediate skill gaps . **Teams Dashboard** monitors performance, and **Cyber Risk Impact Widget** shows how learning reduces breach risk . **CVE Simulations** provide realistic threat-breaking exercises observed by Cybrary's Threat Intelligence Team . **Purple Team Exercises** recreate threat campaigns from **APT35, Royal, APT41** and more . **Enterprise tier** adds **dedicated Customer Success Manager, SSO, LMS integration, Data API, and unlimited custom paths** . Teams plan starts at **$79/month** .



- **[Immersive Labs](https://www.immersivelabs.com/)**  

  **People-centric cybersecurity** platform with **AI-powered Lab Builder** (July 2025) for **real-time lab generation** . **Theory Labs** build foundational knowledge; **Practical Labs** use **organization's own tools and environments** to validate competency . **AI Scenario Generator** (November 2024) creates **threat scenarios for crisis simulations** . Maps to internal standards for **audit-ready theory labs** . **Prove, Improve, Benchmark, and Report (PIBR)** approach unifies Labs, Cyber Drills, Exercises, and Simulations .



- **[SANS OnDemand](https://www.sans.org/)**  

  **The premium cybersecurity training choice** with **$5,000-$7,000 per self-paced course** . Includes **four months of access by default**, with **extended access at $500-$1,500** depending on course and duration . **Virtual lab environments** (NetWars, CyberLive) available with **additional fees** . **Bundling live and OnDemand formats** often achieves **below-list pricing** . **Mixing formats** within a single contract is common for organizations balancing high-priority certifications with broader team enablement .



- **[RangeForce (Cyberbit)](https://www.cyberbit.com/rangeforce/)**  

  **The platform that hosted NATO's Locked Shields**—the first live-fire cyber resilience exercise in 2015 . **Acquired by Cyberbit (September 2025)** to unite cyber range and skills platforms . **Readiness Pathways** generate skills plans for specific roles, team sizes, risks, and threats . **Solo range and labs** provide **hundreds of hours** of individual content from basics to APT groups . **Team exercises** let **blue teams of up to 10** fight full breaches in live environments . **Performance data capture** for both technical and soft skills .



- **[SimSpace](https://simspace.com/)**  

  **The cyber range platform for modern defense** with **layer 3 user and attack emulation**, **layer 2 security stack and apps**, and **layer 1 network and OS architecture** . **Range Workbench** provides drag-and-drop design with **YAML versioning** for infrastructure-as-code principles . **ARIA** intelligent range generation modifies environments using **simple language**—network topology, users, attack paths, and defensive controls . **Tool Tester** validates tools and configurations without production risk . **Team Trainer** plans and repeats **mission-driven exercises** . Trusted by **Fortune 500 organizations** .



- **[Pentester Academy](https://www.pentesteracademy.com/)**  

  **Hands-on cybersecurity training** with **on-demand bootcamps** and **2,000+ labs** covering **130+ subtopics** . **Web Application Security Bootcamp** teaches **OWASP Top 10** vulnerabilities with **50+ lab exercises** across **12+ hours of recordings** . Covers **HTTP protocol basics**, **reconnaissance**, **Burp Suite, ZAP, sqlmap, Nikto, OpenVAS**, and **real-world attack case studies** . **Annual subscription** includes all on-demand bootcamps and labs .



- **[Mandiant Advantage](https://www.mandiant.com/advantage)**  

  **Threat intelligence, attack surface management, and security validation** platform . **MCP (Mandiant Certified Professional)** score uses machine learning to determine if indicators are likely malicious based on **global prevalence, FireEye appliance sightings, OSINT, and IR data** . **APIs drive the entire user interface**—integration partners can build on all data . **Mandiant Academy** offers courses in **Threat Intelligence, Incident Response, Malware Analysis, and Red Teaming** . **ThreatSpace** provides **real-world attack scenario** cyber range exercises .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Ghidra](https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency/ghidra)**  

  **NSA's open-source software reverse engineering framework** (Apache-2.0) . **The most comprehensive open-source RE suite**—includes **disassembler, decompiler, graphing, and scripting** for **Windows, macOS, and Linux** . Supports **a wide variety of processor instruction sets and executable formats**, runs in **user-interactive and automated modes**, and allows **custom extensions via Java or Python** . Built to solve **scaling and teaming problems** in complex SRE efforts and to provide a **customizable and extensible SRE research platform** . **The reference implementation for open-source reverse engineering**.



- **[Übungszentrum Netzverteidigung 2.0 (BSI)](https://www.bsi.bund.de/)**  

  **BSI's open-source cybersecurity training system** (open-source) . **The most significant government-backed open-source training platform**—released March 2026, rebuilt on a modern **Infrastructure-as-Code platform called "CAVE"** . **Five freely available learning modules** covering **OSINT, Web Application Security, Phishing, Malware, and MS Windows Infrastructure Security** . Targets **federal agencies** but also available to **organizations, companies, and universities** . **Community can create and adapt modules**—published on **OpenCode** . The first version (2013) provided the foundation for **IT training, cyber ranges, and security workshops** .



- **[SentinelForge CTF](https://github.com/chadeckles/sentinelforgectf)**  

  **Open-source cyber CTF platform** (open-source) . **Built for modern security professionals**—focuses on **cloud architects, DevOps engineers, platform teams, and security practitioners** rather than traditional ethical hacking . **Challenge categories**: **Trivia** (cloud security knowledge), **Cloud Security** (Azure misconfigurations), **Container Security** (Docker/Kubernetes escapes), **Infrastructure as Code** (Terraform issues), and **DevSecOps** (CI/CD secrets, supply chain) . **5-minute Docker Compose setup** with **PostgreSQL included** . Features **simulated terminals, real-time scoreboard, progressive hints, and first blood bonus** .



- **[PyX64Dbg](https://pypi.org/project/pyx64dbg/)**  

  **Python-based debugger for x86-64 Linux binaries** (open-source) . **The most Pythonic reverse engineering debugger**—uses **Linux ptrace system call and /proc filesystem** to trace and introspect ELF processes . **Three interfaces**: **Python API** for automated RE and binary analysis (unlike GDB, acts as a standard Python library you can import), **GUI** built with PySide6 (disassembly views, memory watches, register panels), and **IPython CLI** for terminal debugging . **Advanced features**: native handling of **PIE, ASLR, shared libraries, and dynamic symbols**, **C-Like Type System** with strict promotion/overflow rules, and **Extended Registers (AVX/SSE)** with YMM0 as float arrays .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Ghidra Skill for AI Agents](https://skillsauth.com/skills/aeondave/ghidra)** — Ghidra integration skill for Claude Code, Cursor, and Windsurf (open-source) . **Enables AI-assisted static analysis** for malware, firmware, and binaries . **Install globally** with one command .

- **[VulnHub](https://www.vulnhub.com/)** — Community-created vulnerable machines for hands-on practice. **The source of PG Play's community machines** .

- Community **n8n** workflows for CTF challenge deployment and training progress notification automation.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Ghidra** for reverse engineering with **PyX64Dbg** for Python-driven debugging, **SentinelForge CTF** for self-hosted challenge platforms, **BSI Übungszentrum** for government-grade training infrastructure, and **VulnHub** for community machine sources—creating a comprehensive open-source cybersecurity training and RE stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Cybersecurity training and reverse engineering tools must be used **lawfully and ethically**. **Reverse engineering proprietary software may violate license agreements**; always verify authorization before analyzing third-party binaries.

- **Open-source reverse engineering is mature and production-grade.** Ghidra is the **NSA's open-source RE framework** with disassembler, decompiler, graphing, and scripting for Windows, macOS, and Linux . PyX64Dbg provides **Python-native debugging** with C-like type systems and AVX/SSE register support . **These are genuine alternatives to commercial RE tools.**

- **Government-backed training infrastructure is now open source.** BSI's **Übungszentrum Netzverteidigung 2.0** (March 2026) is built on a modern IaC platform with **five freely available learning modules**—OSINT, Web App Security, Phishing, Malware, and Windows Infrastructure . **This is a significant milestone for open-source cybersecurity education**—the same infrastructure used by federal agencies is now available to organizations, companies, and universities .

- **The training platform landscape serves different skill levels and budgets.** **TryHackMe** for beginners with gamified learning . **OffSec Proving Grounds** for OSCP+ exam prep with retired exam labs . **Hack The Box Enterprise** for advanced teams with realistic corporate attack surfaces and talent search . **SANS OnDemand** for premium, instructor-led content at **$5,000-$7,000 per course** . **Choose based on your team's maturity and certification goals.**

- **Cyber ranges are the enterprise standard for team readiness.** **RangeForce** hosted the first **Locked Shields** exercise in 2015 and was **acquired by Cyberbit** in September 2025 . **SimSpace** provides **live-fire exercises with authentic payloads** and **AI range generation via ARIA** . **The shift from individual labs to team exercises mirrors real SOC operations**—defending against live attacks with SIEM and XDR tools requires coordination, not just individual skill .

- **Reverse engineering requires appropriate toolchains.** Ghidra provides the **disassembler and decompiler**; PyX64Dbg provides **Python-scriptable debugging** for x86-64 Linux binaries . **The AI-assisted Ghidra skill** enables Claude Code and Cursor to assist with static analysis . **Combine tools based on the target platform and analysis goal.**

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper infrastructure, isolation, and ongoing maintenance. **Training environments containing vulnerable machines must be network-isolated** to prevent accidental exposure. **Reverse engineering malware should be performed in sandboxed environments** with no network access to production systems.
